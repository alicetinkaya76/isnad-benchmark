ISNAD v2 FREEZE — PROVENANCE (added in the second revision round, on reviewer request)
======================================================================================
- The v2 freeze (see freeze_manifest.txt) was executed on 1 June 2026.
- The 31 ATTRIB_FAKE title corrections recorded in the manifest were flagged by an
  automated normalized-title collision audit (a corrected fake title must be absent
  from the corpus title inventory, from the scholar's own authored set, and from all
  other ATTRIB_FAKE items) and regenerated deterministically with a fixed seed (42);
  see runners/02c_freeze_benchmark_v2.py.
- The correction criterion references only the title inventory, never any model
  output; it is therefore blind to model behavior by construction. The changes were
  executed and approved by the author (the manuscript has a single author).
- At freeze time, 9,990 result rows from five models existed against the v1 items.
  The 155 rows referencing the 31 corrected items were deleted; the remaining 9,835
  rows answer items that are bit-identical between v1 and v2 and were retained. The
  targeted re-collection of the 155 rows, and all primary-condition runs, completed
  by 3 June 2026.
- The first analysis of v2 results ran on 4 June 2026. The v2 freeze therefore
  precedes every analysis reported in the manuscript and constitutes the
  exploratory/confirmatory boundary.
- The full output ledger (tables/supplement/r15_output_ledger.csv, Table S28)
  decomposes the released 64,216 raw records, including retained pre-retry rows and
  the aborted 282-row gemma4:31b partial run (excluded from all analyses).
